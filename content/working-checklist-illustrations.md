The working checklist on three A4 pages: the daily loop to pin up, the setup worksheet to fill in once per goal, and the warning signs.
Blue, heavy marks are _the move you make_; grey, thin marks are _the situation around it_. Shapes are schematic, not measured; printed numbers come from the checklist.
